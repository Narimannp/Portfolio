Window-to-Wall Ratio Automation

Automated façade area quantification, glazing ratio analysis, elevation visualization, and drawing-sheet generation directly from the BIM model.

The challenge

Window-to-wall ratio analysis traditionally involves isolating building façades, measuring exterior wall and glazing areas, calculating ratios for each orientation, and manually preparing elevations and summary documentation. Repeating this process as the design changes can be time-consuming and inconsistent.

The solution

A model-driven automation workflow identifies and isolates exterior walls for each side of the building, calculates their areas and the corresponding glazing areas, aggregates the quantities by orientation, and calculates the window-to-wall ratio. It then creates façade-specific views, overlays filled regions to visualize measured areas, and assembles the views and summary table on a drawing sheet.

Window-to-Wall Ratio (per building side)

WWR (%) = Glazing Area ÷ Exterior Wall Area × 100

Area conventions and treatment of openings are defined by project measurement requirements.

Automated workflow

01Identify building orientations and isolate exterior walls on each side
02Calculate wall surface area for each isolated façade
03Identify associated glazing and calculate glazing area
04Aggregate wall and glazing areas by building side
05Calculate window-to-wall ratios and populate a summary table
06Create a dedicated elevation view for each building side
07Generate filled regions to visualize wall and glazing areas in each view
08Create a drawing sheet and place the elevation views and summary table

Deliverables

Orientation-based wall and glazing quantities, calculated window-to-wall ratios, a consolidated analysis table, color-differentiated façade area visualizations, and a coordinated sheet containing the generated views and table.
