Project case study

Predictive Analysis of Construction Change Orders

Planning-stage change-order severity prediction and phase-based analysis of cost behavior.

Predictive analytics · HCPI · Feature selection · Markov chains
01 / Problem

The challenge

Change orders introduce construction cost uncertainty. At the planning stage, detailed designs and job descriptions may not be available, making early risk assessment difficult.

02 / Goal

The objective

Predict change-order severity using accessible project characteristics and investigate how change-cost impacts relate across project phases.

03 / Approach

Method & contribution

  1. Prepare historical change-order records and address data-quality challenges.
  2. Use project type, industry group, size, and spatial features rather than detailed drawings or job descriptions.
  3. Develop Historical Change Performance Indicator (HCPI) attributes to represent domain-informed historical change behavior.
  4. Investigate features using PCA, chi-squared, entropy, and permutation analysis.
  5. Predict severity using high, medium, and low change-impact categories.
  6. Model phase-based temporal dependence with a first-order Markov chain; test Chapman–Kolmogorov relationships across project-duration groups.
  7. Analyze both cumulative and individual periodic change magnitudes.
04 / Findings

Research findings

The research identified predictive value in historical change-performance attributes, spatial features, and feature selection. Sequential analysis supported a Markov-chain representation of change-order dependence. Detailed validation charts and final performance tables will be added with the research outputs.
