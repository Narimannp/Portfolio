AreaPlan

An end-to-end BIM workflow that identifies building boundaries, rooms and units; classifies finished and unfinished areas; calculates and visualizes floor-area metrics; and automatically produces area plans, analytical tables and coordinated presentation sheets for design-option and layout-alternative evaluation.

C# / .NETRevit APIWPF / MVVMArea PlansSchedulesSheet Automation

The challenge

Preparing consistent area documentation requires repeated view creation, room identification, area classification, calculations, visualization, schedules and manual sheet alignment. AreaPlan is designed to coordinate these operations through one reproducible workflow.

Area definitions and regulatory context

AreaPlan supports the measurement and documentation needed for building-area and floor-area review. In Ontario, the 2024 Ontario Building Code, Division A, Article 1.4.1.2, defines terms including building area and floor area. These are distinct from finished/unfinished classifications and from municipal zoning measures such as floor-space index and lot coverage, which may have different calculation rules. The tool can help prepare consistent area documentation, but code or zoning compliance must be verified against the applicable project requirements; automated compliance checking is not claimed here.

Implementation notes

Architecture specification, not a claim that the full production tool is implemented. Revit Area elements and Rooms require explicit mapping rules. Area-plan creation requires an AreaScheme and Level. Viewports and schedules have different sheet placement APIs. Separate Revit transactions are coordinated by the processor.
